Privacy policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy covers the “How It Works” application, a personal tool used by the author of the How It Works channel to publish the channel's own videos.

Who uses the application

Only its author. The application runs on its author's own computer. It has no public version, no sign-up and no other users.

Google data the application accesses

The application requests a single Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses it only to upload videos made by How It Works to the How It Works YouTube channel and to set their title, description, tags and scheduled publication time. It does not read, collect or analyse any other data: no viewer data, no comments, no statistics, no data from other channels, no Gmail, Drive or contacts.

How the data is stored

The authorization token and the identifiers of the uploaded videos (used to avoid uploading the same video twice) are stored only on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server of ours: there is none.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The videos are sent only to the platforms where they are published (YouTube, and the channel's own Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts).

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The application also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The locally stored token is then deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy: through the About section of the How It Works YouTube channel.
