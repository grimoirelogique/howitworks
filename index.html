How It Works

Everyday things, cut open & explained.

How It Works is a channel of short animated videos (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok) that explain how everyday objects work: locks, zippers, headphones, microwaves, GPS, watches, touchscreens… Every episode is drawn in its own art style and its facts are checked before publication.

The How It Works publishing application

The “How It Works” application is a personal publishing tool. It runs only on its author's own computer and is used by its author alone, to upload the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts and to schedule their publication. It has no public sign-up, no other users and no server.

For YouTube, it requests a single Google permission, youtube.upload, used only to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel with a scheduled publication time.
